Band List setup

Takes about 5 minutes, once. Ask in the group chat for the band passcode.

1. Add the app to your home screen

  1. Open this link in Safari:
    Open Band List
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Band List from your home screen. Enter your name, the band passcode, and where you listen (Apple Music, Spotify or YouTube Music).

2. Make the “Add to Band List” share button

This lets you share a song straight from Apple Music, Spotify or YouTube.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to make a new shortcut. Name it Add to Band List.
  2. Tap the ⓘ (info) button at the bottom and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Tap Done.
  3. At the top it now says “Receive Images and 18 more from Share Sheet”. Tap that blue text, tap Clear, then tick only URLs and Text.
  4. Tap where it says “If there’s no input: Continue” and change it to Get Clipboard.
  5. Search for the action URL Encode and add it. It should say “URL Encode Shortcut Input”.
  6. Search for the action Open URLs and add it. Tap its blue URL field and type:
    https://christinechancky.github.io/tmb-playlist/?add=
    then, straight after the =, tap URL Encoded Text from the suggestions bar above the keyboard.
  7. Tap Done.

3. Use it

  1. In Apple Music, Spotify or YouTube, tap Share on a song.
  2. Scroll the row of apps or actions and pick Add to Band List. (First time: tap Edit Actions and add it to Favourites so it stays near the top.)
  3. The Add song screen opens with the link filled in. Pick tags, tap Add to Wishlist.
The first time the shortcut opens the app, it opens in Safari, which counts as a separate browser from your home-screen icon. Enter your name and passcode once more there; after that it’s remembered.

Good to know

  • “Probably on the list as …” means a song with the same name is already there. Skip keeps the existing one; Add anyway adds yours as a separate version.
  • YouTube only songs (covers etc.) open on YouTube for everyone, no account needed.
  • no link yet songs were typed into the sheet by hand. Tapping play searches your music app instead.
  • Everything you add lands in the band’s Google Sheet.
